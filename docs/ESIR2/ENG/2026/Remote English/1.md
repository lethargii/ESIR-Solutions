## Hüsker Dü

Today, I'm going to talk about Hüsker Dü. It is not very known. I would say that if I asked any stranger about the band there is little chance that he would even have heard of it. Yet, it is actually a very VERY important band. One could argue that Nirvana wouldn't even have existed. So it is very important.

Hüsker Dü is a hardcore punk band. It's the same hard from hard rock and the same punk from punk rock, so it is in fact a mix of both. Their music mainly consists of shouting, hard guitar and bass lines (that's the hardcore part) which are yet very simple (that's the punk part). But one band must never be reduced to one genre as they never do only one thing. It is very obvious on the album I'm going to talk about as there are two songs which are basically piano solos. This is to say that it inspired a lot of bands from the hard rock scene.

Hüsker Dü is composed of three members only, Bob Mould (songwriting, guitar, vocals), Grant Hart (songwriting, drums, vocals) and Greg Norton (bass). How the band was formed is somewhat funny for they met because Mould was buying drugs from Hart. The most important thing to talk about is their leader Bob Mould. As a lot of people he had a bunch of issues which might have been one of the reasons he started to create music. Particularly, he was gay, which, at the time, was not something that could be seen as a good thing. Maybe the song I'm going to talk about is related to one of those issues.

Finally, let's get to the song. I'm going to talk about "Whatever" which is from the experimental double album Zen Arcade, an album which was the main reason Hüsker Dü influenced so much rock bands.

### Analysis couplet by couplet, verse by verse

Let's first, analyse every verse of this song...

`He lives in his imagination with those friends of his very own`  

This song is about a boy whose life seems to mainly consist of what he's creating in his mind. People must know it from the fact that they see him talking with imaginary friends.

`He doesn't get along with the outside world; he'd rather be alone`

We learn that all this might come from the fact that for some reasons living in the real world is hard for him (some people might have hurt him we don't really know) which lead him to isolate himself and build his own world where everything goes the way he wants.

`Sometimes when it's late at night, he starts to wonder why
The plans he made can never happen, so all he does is cry`

Here, we learn that he in fact knows that 'his world' is not real though he wishes it to be real. When that thought crosses his mind, he starts to cry.

`His parents, they can't understand why their son, he turned out wrong`

The boy's parents, who seemingly think it's wrong that their son lives in an imaginary world, don't know how he became the way he is.

`He runs away from all the pain and forgets them when he's gone`

In this verse, we have the confirmation that he was hurt by people from the real world.

`He'd rather be all by himself, because his plans, they seem the best`

Though he might get in touch with the real world he doesn't because his imaginary world seems better than anything.

`He finally gets the nerve one day, now life becomes a test`

One day, he decides to confront the real world even though it is very hard.

`Whatever you want, whatever you do, wherever you go, whatever you say`

This verse represents what the boy thinks about other people. Nobody should decide for him what he must do and nobody should judge him.

`Mom and Dad, I'm sorry
Mom and Dad, don't worry
I'm not the son you wanted, but what could you expect?
I've made my world of happiness to combat your neglect`

This passage is what the boy says to his parents' when he confronts them. He says to his parents that the reason he turned out to be like this is because of them, because they neglected him.

### Further analysis

What can we get from this song?

We can clearly see that their are two parts in the song. The first one is where the boy tries to completely forgets all his troubles by running away in an imaginary world though he's not oblivious to the fact that the world he lives in is made up. The second part is when he gathers up courage and decides to confront the real world and especially his parents who did not help him when they should have. That is the story this song is telling. We don't really know though if it was taken from something that really happened.

I belive the moral of the story is that people can be bad sometimes and it doesn't matter what people think about you or what people tell you to do.
